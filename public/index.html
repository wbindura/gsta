Admission Requirements

  • ✓ Birth certificate (certified copy)
  • ✓ Previous school reports (Forms 1-4) or O-Level results (Forms 5-6)
  • ✓ Medical records and immunization certificate
  • ✓ Two recent passport photos
  • ✓ Proof of residence
  • ✓ Parent/guardian ID copy

Admission Process

  1. Complete online application form
  2. Submit required documents
  3. Pay registration fee ($50)
  4. Attend orientation session
  5. Complete payment for first term
  6. Collect student ID and QR card
